## What's here

The original 55-page `Los Gardios Partner Agreement` (source: the org's
Google Drive, both `.docx` and `.pdf`, Hebrew-authoritative + English
convenience translation) has been split into two documents, per request:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Los_Gardios_Partner_Agreement_HE.docx` / `_EN.docx` | The short, signed contract — status, core obligations (confidentiality, non-solicitation, IP, data protection, timelines), the compensation clause, termination, dispute resolution, signatures. Hebrew is the binding version per its own governing-language clause; English is a convenience translation. |
| `Los_Gardios_Partner_Handbook_HE.docx` / `_EN.docx` | The new, incorporated-by-reference operations manual — onboarding, tier structure, project workflow, performance/growth, compensation mechanics in detail, IP/data-security/decision-making in practice. Not signed separately; a partner acknowledges having read it as part of signing the Agreement. |

Both retain the original's fonts (Frank Ruhl Libre), logo, header/footer
("Confidential" mark, page numbers, parties' initials line), and RTL layout —
built by splicing the original `.docx` XML rather than retyping content, so
formatting is inherited, not recreated.

## The two changes made, and what to check

**1. Structural split.** Everything operational moved to the Handbook.
The Agreement keeps every clause that creates a personal obligation, a
financial term, or a legal right — including the full compensation
Waterfall percentages, the liability cap and its four uncapped carve-outs,
and the complete confidentiality/non-solicitation/IP sections — since
those define what a partner is actually agreeing to. Target was "3-5
pages"; keeping that material intact instead of cutting it runs
noticeably longer once formatted in Word — **this environment can't
render a docx to confirm the exact page count (no working document
renderer available here), so open the file and check.** If it's longer
than you want, the next-safest trims are the Section 1 definitions list
(currently kept in full for cross-reference safety) and Section 13(b)'s
3-tier accrual math — not the compensation, liability-cap, or Waterfall
figures.

The Agreement now incorporates the Handbook by reference (new clause,
inserted after the original amendment section): the Organization can
update the Handbook's operational content with advance written notice,
but not the Agreement's core terms (pay, confidentiality, IP, the
compensation figure) without the partner's written consent.

**2. Fines reframed as investment + consequence.** The original Section
9(f)/(g) ("Liquidated damages — a floor without a cap," ₪100,000 minimum
per breach) is unchanged in legal substance — same figure, same
uncapped/floor structure, same blue-pencil severability — but now opens
with what the Organization actually invests (Neuron OS, SOPs/templates,
training, brand, data-security infrastructure) before stating that
maliciously harming one of those resources carries that compensation.
**If the intent was also to change the amount or narrow it to intentional
conduct only (not the negligence-adjacent triggers the original already
had), that's a substantive legal change, not a framing one — flag it and
it can be adjusted.**

**3. Naming fix worth knowing about.** The original contract already has
a *different*, capitalized, non-incorporated term — "the Source Protocol"
(פרוטוקול המקור, Section 1) — for HQ's internal compensation/tier
framework. Calling the new document "Organization Protocols" would have
collided with it, so it's named the **Partner Handbook** instead, with an
explicit clause distinguishing the two.

**4. Pre-existing issue found, not introduced here:** the *original*
English convenience translation has its own internal lettering drift in
Section 8 (its English sub-clauses don't quite line up with the Hebrew
ones a couple of clauses in) — harmless in the original since Hebrew
governs, but worth a look if the English copy is ever relied on directly.

## Recommended next step

This was restructured by an AI agent, not a lawyer. Given real money
(₪100,000+ per breach), real international partners, and jurisdiction-
specific annexes are involved, **have an Israeli-licensed attorney review
both documents — especially the compensation clause and the Handbook
incorporation-by-reference mechanism — before circulating them for
signature.**
