## Section 1 — Definitions

**"the Agreement"** — the Framework Agreement (Parts I–IV), Appendices A, B and C, each signed Proposal (with respect to the Specific Engagement under which it was signed), Appendix D (to the extent marked as applicable in the relevant signed Proposal, or expressly attached in another manner), the Client Guide in the version and of the date stated on the Signature Page (see Section 15(a1)), and the Supplementary Understandings Appendix (to the extent completed and approved) — together.

**"the Proposal"** — a specific Commercial Engagement Proposal, signed by the Authorized Representatives, setting out the Consideration, the engagement model, the term, the response deadline, the Exit Schedule, the Buyout price and the other economic terms; "**Specific Engagement**" — the activity carried out under one signed Proposal.

**"the Consideration"** — any payment due to the Organization for its services, **excluding** the media budget transferred to the platforms under Section 5(b). **"the Average Monthly Consideration"** — the average monthly Consideration in the 12 months preceding the relevant event, for the Specific Engagement in the course of which the breach occurred (or the actual period of activity of that engagement, if shorter); and absent 12 months of activity for that engagement — the total monthly Consideration stated in the signed Proposals active at the relevant time relating to that Specific Engagement only, and not in all the signed Proposals active between the Parties. **"the Deliverables"** — final work products delivered and approved within a Specific Engagement, the consideration for which has been paid in full.

**"the Organization's Assets"** — the assets listed in Section 7(b), including the Neuron systems (the Organization's proprietary information, technology and artificial intelligence systems) and its accumulated know-how, as described in the Client Guide.

**(a) Response Deadline and Deemed Approval.** The Client shall review each Deliverable and return to the Organization a defined and final response in writing by the response deadline set in the Proposal; absent a stated deadline — three (3) Business Days from the date of delivery of the Deliverable. Nothing in the foregoing prevents the Parties from negotiating and agreeing on adjusted terms in writing at any time, and nothing therein grants a right to unilaterally change the Consideration or the terms of this Agreement, subject to Section 15(b).

**(c) Form of Binding Communication.** Approval of a Deliverable, a professional instruction or a change in the terms of the engagement shall be given in a manner capable of being presented in writing (an email message, a record in the Neuron system, a call or meeting recorded under Appendix C, or a meeting summarized in writing and approved by the representatives of both Parties) — and shall be binding only in that manner.

**(a) Fees and Payment.** The Consideration is paid against a lawful invoice, in full and on the date set in the Proposal; where a payment is in arrears, it shall bear interest at the rate set in the Proposal, and absent such rate — in accordance with the Adjudication of Interest and Linkage Law, 5721-1961. **(f) Prohibition on Unilateral Set-Off.** The Client shall not be entitled to withhold, reduce or set off from the Consideration due to the Organization any amount on account of a claim, demand or action against the Organization, unless it is an amount approved in advance and in writing by the Organization or determined in a final judgment, all subject to the provisions of any law; a dispute regarding part of the services or of the Consideration shall not exempt the Client from paying the undisputed amounts when due.

## Section 8 — Protection of Professional and Business Relationships (Non-Solicitation and Non-Circumvention)

**(a) Protected Relationships.** A representative, employee, manager, shareholder, partner, subcontractor, service provider, expert or consultant of the Organization; and also any supplier, manufacturer, distributor, business partner or other business party — provided that, in both cases, it is a person to whom the Organization actually introduced the Client, between whom the Organization intermediated, or in which relationship formed between the Client and that party the Organization was actually and provably involved, for the purpose of distribution, sale, procurement, manufacturing, service or a business opportunity.

**(b) Non-Solicitation.** The Client shall not solicit, entice or engage, directly or indirectly, a Protected Relationship of the first type, for service or work outside the framework of the Organization.

**(c) Non-Circumvention.** The Client shall not approach a Protected Relationship of the second type for a direct engagement circumventing the Organization, and shall not respond to such an offer — except in advance coordination with, and in writing from, the Organization.

**(d) Limits of the Protection.** Nothing in subsections (b)–(c) prevents an engagement based on a prior independent relationship of the Client, an initiated approach that does not rely on an introduction through the Organization, an initiated approach by that party that does not stem from such an introduction, general and publicly available knowledge, or the employment of a person who responded to a generally published position.

**(e) Term.** The obligations apply during the term of the engagement and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter.

**(f) Cure of Breach.** A curable breach — including an approach that has not yet matured into an actual engagement — shall not give rise to a right to liquidated damages if the Client has ceased it, has given written notice of its cancellation and has derived no benefit from it, within seven (7) Business Days of the Organization's notice; the right to cure does not arise where the breach has been completed, where granting additional time would increase the damage, or in a repeated breach of the same obligation.

**(g) Means of Proving a Protected Relationship.** For the purpose of proving the existence of a Protected Relationship as set out in subsection (a), the Organization may rely, among other things, on correspondence, commercial proposals, minutes, records in its systems and any other documentation evidencing its actual involvement in creating the relationship or the business opportunity.

The Schedule forms an integral part of this subsection and of the Framework Agreement, and enters into force upon signature on the Signature Page as do all other parts of the Agreement.

The Client Guide, Chapter 1, under the heading "Foundations of the Cost of Protecting the Organization's Assets", describes by way of illustration the cost categories and orders of magnitude on the basis of which the amounts in the Schedule were set; nothing in the foregoing creates a calculation formula to be re-applied at the time of the breach, nor does it derogate from the finality of the amounts set in the Schedule or from the principle that no proof of actual damage is required. The cure period of seven (7) Business Days in this subsection is intended for determining entitlement to liquidated damages only, and is separate from the period of fifteen (15) Business Days for the purpose of the termination right in Section 13(c); curing a breach within the period in Section 13(c) may prevent termination of the Agreement, but does not derogate from liquidated damages that have already arisen under this subsection if the breach was not cured within the shorter period. Compensation for a breach of Section 9 (confidentiality) is due to the injured Party — Client or Organization alike — in accordance with the mutual duty of confidentiality there; compensation for a breach of Sections 8 and 10(b) is due to the Organization only, in accordance with the fact that the obligations in those Sections apply to the Client only. "Event", for the purpose of this subsection only — each Protected Relationship, item of Confidential Information or separate asset that has been harmed; a series of acts relating to the same relationship, item or asset — including repeated acts — constitutes one event, entitling to the highest liquidated damages among the rows applicable to it only, and not to their aggregate.

Nothing in the foregoing derogates, cumulatively, from a non-monetary remedy (injunction, return of an asset, deletion of information, termination of the Agreement) or from compensation for separate and distinct damage that is not the damage for which the liquidated damages were paid.

**(c) Aggregate Cap and Supplementary Provisions.** The total liquidated damages due to each Party under subsection (a), over the entire term of the Agreement, shall not exceed the higher of ₪600,000 or 18× the Average Monthly Consideration. Each Party shall act reasonably to mitigate its damage; where an amount or mechanism is found to be unenforceable or disproportionate as drafted, it shall be adjusted to the minimum extent necessary only and shall not be voided. This Section is the sole mechanism setting liquidated damages, except for the Completion Payment under Appendix A, Section 1(c).

## Section 12 — Limitation of Liability (Mutual)

# Appendix D — Personal Data Protection (Personal Data Processing Agreement)

> This Appendix applies to the engagement **only if marked as such in the relevant signed Proposal**, or expressly attached in another manner. If marked or attached — this Appendix is an integral part of the Agreement, and prevails over Appendix C in matters of personal data only. If not marked — it does not apply.

## Section 1 — Definitions and Application

**"Personal data"**, **"data subject"**, **"controller"** and **"holder (processor)"** — as defined in the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (as amended); **"Controller"**, **"Processor"** and **"Data Subject"** — as defined in the GDPR, to the extent applicable. **"Sub-processor"** — as set out in Section 6 below. This Appendix elaborates and supplements Appendix C with respect to personal data; in any matter not expressly regulated herein, Appendix C shall continue to apply.

## Section 2 — Purposes and Manner of Processing

**(a)** The Organization shall process personal data solely for the purpose of providing the services, in accordance with the Client's documented instructions — except for data that has passed into Layer C, which is not subject to this Appendix.

**(b)** Where the Organization considers that a processing instruction breaches data protection law — it shall notify the Client in writing without delay, and may suspend its performance until it has been clarified.

## Section 3 — Types of Data and Data Subjects

Contact and identification details of the Client's representatives/employees, and of the Client's customers to the extent exposed to them for the purpose of the services; and usage and behavioral data collected in the course of marketing activity for the Client. Where processing of a materially additional type is required — the Parties shall update in writing.

## Section 4 — Confidentiality of Processing Personnel

The Organization shall ensure that every person authorized on its behalf to process personal data is bound by confidentiality no less stringent than that in Section 9 of the Framework Agreement.

## Section 5 — Information Security

The security measures in Appendix C, Section 3(a), adapted to the sensitivity of the personal data — access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, and role-based permission management.

## Section 6 — Sub-Processors

**(a)** The Client authorizes the Organization to engage sub-processors of the types described in Appendix C, Section 3(b), bound in writing by obligations no less stringent than those in this Appendix. **(b)** Upon request, the Organization shall provide the list of sub-processors; where it adds a material new sub-processor — it shall give advance notice within a reasonable time.

## Section 7 — International Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside Israel, the Organization shall ensure compliance with the conditions of the Protection of Privacy Regulations (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad), 5761-2001, including a written undertaking by the data recipient to the extent required; where data subject to the GDPR is transferred outside the European Union, the Organization shall also ensure an appropriate safeguard under that law (standard contractual clauses, an adequacy decision or an equivalent mechanism). The Organization shall inform the Client of the transfer mechanism upon its request.

## Section 8 — Assistance to the Client and Exercise of Data Subject Rights

Where a data subject has submitted a request for access/correction/deletion/objection/portability — the Organization shall refer them to the Client and assist in responding within a reasonable time, no later than seven (7) Business Days.

## Section 9 — Security Incident and Reporting

Where the Organization has discovered an incident of breach, or a suspected breach, of the security of personal data — it shall notify the Client in writing without unreasonable delay and no later than 48 hours, and shall assist the Client in fulfilling its reporting obligations, without itself approaching the authority/affected persons on the Client's behalf without coordination, unless required by law.

## Section 10 — Deletion and Return upon Termination

At the Client's written election, the Organization shall return or delete the personal data, subject to retention obligations under law and to routine backup copies (Appendix C, Section 3(d)); where the Client has not made an election within thirty (30) days of termination — the Organization shall delete.

## Section 11 — Audit and Oversight

Upon written demand and reasonable prior notice, the Organization shall provide reasonable documentation demonstrating its compliance with this Appendix; upon reasonable suspicion of a material breach or a regulatory audit — an additional audit by prior coordination, not more than once a year under ordinary circumstances.

## Section 12 — Liability

The Parties' liability for a breach of this Appendix is subject in full to the mechanisms of Sections 6 and 12 of the Framework Agreement; a breach of this Appendix that also constitutes a breach of Sections 8–10 of the Framework Agreement (for example, a breach of confidentiality) shall additionally entitle to liquidated damages under Section 11, in accordance with its terms. Nothing in this Appendix creates a separate or unlimited track of liability, except to the extent that mandatory law requires otherwise.
